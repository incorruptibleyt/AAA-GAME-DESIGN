# Bone Chain privacy policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

Bone Chain is a dungeon game for Android made by incorruptibleyt. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

## The short version

Bone Chain has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it never collects your name, email, contacts, location or advertising ID. To help fix bugs and balance the game, it sends short **anonymous game reports** to the developer. You can switch this off at any time in the pause menu (**Send reports: Off**).

## What the game reports

When a run ends, when the game hits an error, or when it didn't close properly last time, the game sends a short text report containing:

- **The run summary:** hero, floor and room reached, time, score, kills, coins, damage taken and what dealt it, blessings and Armory upgrades, and frame rate. This is the same text as the **COPY RUN REPORT** button.
- **Error and crash details:** the error message, where in the game it happened, and the last lines of the game's own log.
- **A random install number** made on your phone the first time you play. It is not linked to you, your Google account or your phone's advertising ID, and it changes if you reinstall.
- **Technical details:** the game version, phone model, Android version and graphics chip name.

Reports are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a Google Form owned by the developer, and stored in the developer's Google account (Google acts as a service provider). They are used only to fix bugs and balance the game. They are never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. Reports are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.

If you're offline, up to 20 reports wait on your phone and are sent later. Turning reports off stops new ones being sent.

## What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress (coins, unlocked heroes, Armory upgrades, best floor and score, settings and simple play counts such as perfect dodges) in a file on your own phone. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** only to send the anonymous game reports described above.
- **Vibrate:** the phone buzzes briefly when your hero is hit.

The game asks for no other permissions. It cannot see your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

## Deleting reports

Because reports are anonymous, the developer can't tell which ones are yours unless you share your install number. To have your reports deleted, email the developer (address on the Google Play page) with the install number, which is shown at the top of every report you copy with **COPY RUN REPORT**.

## Children

Bone Chain is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version collects anything else (for example ads or purchases), this policy will be updated first and the Google Play data safety section will say so.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address listed on the game's Google Play page.
