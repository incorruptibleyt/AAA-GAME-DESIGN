# Bone Chain privacy policy

_Last updated: November 3, 2026_

Bone Chain is a dungeon game for Android made by incorruptibleyt. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

## The short version

Bone Chain has no accounts and no ads, and it never collects your name, email, contacts, location or advertising ID. To help fix bugs and balance the game, it can send short **anonymous game reports** to the developer, but **only if you say yes**. Reports are off until then. The game asks you once, when you open it (or at the end of a run, if you started playing before answering), and you can change your answer at any time in the pause menu (**Send reports**). You can also type a note to the developer with **Send feedback** in the pause menu. There is one optional purchase, the **Crypt Pass**, which is paid for through Google Play.

## What the game reports

If you have turned reports on, then when a run ends, when the game hits an error, when it didn't close properly last time, or when you open the game (at most once a day), the game sends a short text report containing:

- **The run summary:** hero, floor and room reached, time, score, kills, coins, damage taken and what dealt it, blessings and Armory upgrades, and frame rate. This is the same text as the **COPY REPORT** button.
- **The daily opening note:** how many times you've opened the game, whether you've finished the tutorial, your best floor, how many runs you've won, whether you own the Crypt Pass, and which hero you've picked.
- **Error and crash details:** the error message, where in the game it happened (including how long the game had been open), and the last lines of the game's own log.
- **A random install number** made on your phone the first time you play. It is not linked to you, your Google account or your phone's advertising ID, and it changes if you reinstall.
- **Technical details:** the game version, phone model, Android version, graphics chip name, how much memory the phone has, and how much memory the game is using.

**Feedback you type:** if you open **Send feedback** in the pause menu and tap **SEND**, your note is sent the same way, with the random install number, the technical details above and where you were in the game. This happens even if reports are off, because you chose to send it. Please don't include your name, email or other personal details in the note.

Reports are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a Google Form owned by the developer, and stored in the developer's Google account (Google acts as a service provider). They are used only to fix bugs and balance the game. They are never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. Reports are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.

If you're offline, up to 20 reports wait on your phone and are sent later. Turning reports off stops new ones being sent and deletes any still waiting on your phone. With reports off, the game sends nothing except a note you choose to send with **Send feedback**.

## The Crypt Pass (in-app purchase)

The Crypt Pass is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the whole payment, so the game never sees your card, payment details, name or email. The game only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the Pass, and saves the answer on your phone so it works offline. No order or payment details are included in the anonymous reports. Google's own privacy policy covers the payment.

## What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress (coins, unlocked heroes, Armory upgrades, best floor and score, Daily Crypt best scores, chosen Curses, Curse rewards, free hero trials used, whether you own the Crypt Pass, settings and simple play counts such as perfect dodges) in a file on your own phone, with a backup copy in case the phone shuts down while saving. A run in progress is saved in another file on your phone so you can continue it. All of these files are deleted when you uninstall the game.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** only to send the anonymous game reports described above (if you've turned them on) and feedback you choose to send.
- **Vibrate:** the phone buzzes briefly when your hero is hit (switch it off under Pause, Vibration).
- **Google Play billing:** only to buy the Crypt Pass through Google Play.
- **Network state:** added by Google Play billing, to check whether the phone is online before talking to Google Play. It can't see what you do online.

The game asks for no other permissions. It cannot see your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

## Deleting reports

Because reports are anonymous, the developer can't tell which ones are yours unless you share your install number. To have your reports deleted, email the developer at egence9965@gmail.com with your install number. It is shown in the pause menu under the build number, whether reports are on or off, and at the top of every report you copy with **COPY REPORT**.

## Children

Bone Chain is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version collects anything else (for example ads), this policy will be updated first and the Google Play data safety section will say so.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to delete your reports, can be sent to the developer at **egence9965@gmail.com**.
