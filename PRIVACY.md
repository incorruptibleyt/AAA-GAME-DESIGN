# Bone Chain privacy policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

Bone Chain is a dungeon game for Android made by incorruptibleyt. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

## The short version

Bone Chain does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not use the internet.

## What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress (coins, unlocked heroes, Armory upgrades, best floor, settings and simple play counts such as perfect dodges) in a file on your own phone. Nobody else can see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

## Permissions

- **Vibrate:** the phone buzzes briefly when your hero is hit. Nothing else uses this permission.

The game asks for no other permissions. It cannot see your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

## The run report button

At the end of a run you can tap **COPY RUN REPORT**, which copies a text summary of that run (hero, floors, damage numbers, frame rate) to your clipboard. It only goes anywhere if you choose to paste it somewhere yourself.

## Children

Bone Chain is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects data (for example ads, purchases or crash reports), this policy will be updated first and the Google Play data safety section will say so.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address listed on the game's Google Play page.
