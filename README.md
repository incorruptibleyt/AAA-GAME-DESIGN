# Bone Chain

A top-down dungeon action game for Android phones, built in **Godot 4.7**. Fight down through a crypt
one room at a time: drag your thumb to move, stand still to shoot, pick a blessing after every room,
and face the Skeleton King at the end of the floor.

**New here? Start with [SETUP.md](SETUP.md).** Everything works from a phone. You never need a computer.

## What's in this first playable slice

- **One floor:** 5 rooms (2 easy, 2 medium, 1 hard, picked at random from 36 hand-made rooms) plus a boss room. From floor 2 a room may take its right-hand side from another room (every room is drawn symmetric, so the result is lopsided and new), and from floor 3 the far half of one room may be joined to the near half of another. Floor 1 never has more than one skeleton mage in a wave.
- **Four heroes:** the Rogue (crossbow), plus the Knight (armoured sword waves), Barbarian (thrown axes, heals on kills) and Mage (exploding orbs), unlocked with coins from the HEROES screen. It shows one hero at a time turning on a pedestal (drag to spin them), with bars for health, damage, attack speed and speed, their special, and the three blessings only they can find. Each hero's level-20 Mastery aura has its own colour and shape: shadow daggers circling the Rogue (violet), a lion's mane of gold flame round the Knight, lightning leaping off the Barbarian to strike the floor (cyan), and gold stars running round two tilted rings round the Mage (green); see Fx.motif in [scripts/fx.gd](scripts/fx.gd).
- **Controls:** one-thumb floating joystick, auto-aim and auto-fire when standing still, a quick flick of the thumb or the DASH button (bottom right) to dash (untouchable while dashing; the button dashes the way the stick pointed in the last half second, so one thumb can let go and tap it), brief invulnerability after a hit. Android Back pauses a run, goes to the title from the death and victory screens (a won run stays saved for CONTINUE), and leaves the game from the title.
- **Four skeletons.** The dangerous ones show a red warning before they attack:
  - *Minion*: rushes you in packs.
  - *Warrior*: shows a red strip, then lunges along it.
  - *Archer*: keeps its distance and fires aimed arrows.
  - *Mage*: marks a circle under you, then blows it up.
  - Each floor theme brings its own skeleton (from the second run): on Frozen Ossuary floors a *Frost Wraith* (a hooded ghost floating in a ragged cloak, ice shards circling it) wails, marks a line and fires an ice beam down it that slows you, and in the Ember Catacomb a *Cinder Skeleton* (charred bones with glowing cracks, black horns and a burning skull) runs at you leaving patches of fire behind. In the Starfall Abyss (version 269, [scripts/enemies/void_walker.gd](scripts/enemies/void_walker.gd)) a *Void Walker* (bones of night sky, a cape of stars, a crescent-moon blade and a crescent moon floating over its skull) sinks into a rift at its feet; a second rift opens on the floor right beside you, and a second later it steps out of it swinging. Walls and cover don't stop it, and it can't be hit while it is in the void: get away from the new rift. In the Clockwork Foundry (version 270, [scripts/enemies/clockwork_skeleton.gd](scripts/enemies/clockwork_skeleton.gd)) a *Clockwork Skeleton* (bones dark as old iron, a big brass wind-up key in its back, a glowing cog on its chest and a smokestack on its skull) stops while its key whirs round and lights up its path: a strip through you to the wall, then the strip it will bounce along. It charges the whole path, then stands run down for a moment, key still, taking half as much again from every hit. In the Whispering Library (version 271, [scripts/enemies/hush_keeper.gd](scripts/enemies/hush_keeper.gd) and [scripts/hush_circle.gd](scripts/hush_circle.gd)) a *Hush Keeper* (a skeleton librarian, its bones written all over in glowing script, a candle burning on its skull, a great open book floating before it with its pages turning, and a quill in its hand) keeps its distance and writes: a circle of letters writes itself round the floor where you stand, one letter after another, and once the ring closes the floor inside goes dark as spilt ink and your weapon falls silent while you stay in it. Step out to fight again, or kill the Hush Keeper and its circle fades at once. In the Amethyst Mine (version 272, [scripts/enemies/geode_guard.gd](scripts/enemies/geode_guard.gd)) a *Geode Guard* (a big miner skeleton dusted with rock and flecked with amethyst, a miner's helmet with a lamp, a pick, and half a great geode on its arm, the crystals turned outwards) blocks every shot that hits it from the front, and the Knight's sword too. It turns slowly and its lamp lights the floor the way it faces, so walk round it and hit its side or back. Up close it raises its pick and slams crystals up out of the floor along a strip in front of it; then the pick sticks and the geode swings aside for a moment, open from every side and taking more from every hit. In the Sandfall Tomb (version 273, [scripts/enemies/tomb_mummy.gd](scripts/enemies/tomb_mummy.gd)) a *Tomb Mummy* (a skeleton wound in old linen from skull to toe, strips torn away to the bone and loose ends trailing, a gold circlet on its brow, a broad collar of turquoise and gold and a glowing turquoise scarab on its chest, a roll of bandage in its hand) shambles after you and throws the loose end of its bandage: a strip on the floor shows where, then the bandage lashes out along it. Step off the strip. If it catches you, the bandage holds you on a leash that it reels in, and when it has you close it strikes. Dash to snap the bandage: it tears, and the mummy staggers back.
- **The Skeleton King:** bone rings, a charge, meteor circles and summoning. He gets faster below half health.
- **The Sand Pharaoh** (version 274, [scripts/enemies/sand_pharaoh.gd](scripts/enemies/sand_pharaoh.gd) and [scripts/sand_mound.gd](scripts/sand_mound.gd)): the Sandfall Tomb's own boss, the last of the free game (floor 5 had the Skeleton King and the Lich together before). A giant mummy king: linen wound round its bones with bands of gold, a striped headcloth of lapis and gold with a cobra on the brow, a false beard, a broad collar and a winged scarab, the crook and flail crossed on its chest and a sun disc floating over its head (the colossus heads in the Tomb are its statues). Royal Bandages (three strips fan out, then its wrappings lash out along them; caught, you are reeled in to be struck, and a dash snaps the bandage), Sandfall (sand pours from the ceiling on you and round you, each pour leaving a mound that slows you while you wade through it until it sinks again), Scarab Swarm (streams of scarabs pour out of it in turning spokes; keep to the gaps) and, below half health, Sunbeam (the sun burns a beam along the floor that sweeps round the room; dash through it or hide behind cover) as two Tomb Mummies climb out of the sand.
- **The Crystal Tyrant** (version 275, [scripts/enemies/crystal_tyrant.gd](scripts/enemies/crystal_tyrant.gd) and [scripts/crystal_spire.gd](scripts/crystal_spire.gd)): the Amethyst Mine's own boss (floor 7 had the Skeleton King before). The skeleton that dug too deep: amethyst grown through its bones, a crown of crystals burst from its skull, a ridge of great crystals along its back and a fist of raw crystal. Crystal Garden (circles mark the floor on you and round you, then spires of amethyst burst up: while they stand they are solid, hemming you in but stopping shots, carts and ripples too, and each cracks after a few seconds, its shards flying out along strips), Runaway Carts (strips run across the room, then mine carts heaped with crystal thunder out of the side tunnels along them; step between them or behind cover, where a cart smashes), Crystal Ripple (it raises its fist with a ring of crystals round its feet and slams the floor: the ring runs out across the room with gaps in it; stand in a gap, dash through it or keep a pillar between you) and, below half health, Crystal Prison (pillars rise round you one by one, closing towards the last gap; get out before it shuts and bursts inwards) as two Geode Guards climb out of the rock.
- **The Archivist** (version 276, [scripts/enemies/archivist.gd](scripts/enemies/archivist.gd) and [scripts/enemies/ink_double.gd](scripts/enemies/ink_double.gd)): the Whispering Library's own boss (floor 8 had the Lich, the boss of floor 2, back again). The keeper of the forbidden books: a tall skeleton scholar in a square cap with a gold tassel, great round spectacles glowing with the whispering, an ink-blue stole, a padlocked tome swinging from its fist on a chain and wings of loose pages fanned out from its back, more pages circling it. It reads, and what it reads comes true. Burning Words (a word writes itself across the floor where you stand, stroke by stroke, in letters taller than you, then burns; step into a gap inside a letter or out of the word), Retelling (the steps you took in the last few seconds glow as inky footprints and burst one after another, chasing you along the way you came; don't turn back or stand still), Bookstorm (books burst out of the shelves on the walls and flap across the room at you along strips) and, below half health, Mirror Page (it tears a page from its tome and your own ink double climbs out across the room: it copies your every step as in a mirror, hurts to touch and throws ink back when you stand still to fight; break it, or it dries up) as its pages blaze and two Hush Keepers step out of the stacks.
- **The Forgemaster** (version 281, [scripts/enemies/forgemaster.gd](scripts/enemies/forgemaster.gd)): the Clockwork Foundry's own boss (floor 9 had the Cinder Colossus, the boss of floor 3, before). The master smith of the works: a giant of riveted iron bones with a furnace burning behind a grate in its ribs, a welding mask with a glowing slit for a face, smokestacks puffing on its shoulders, a great brass clock wheel turning behind its head and a forge hammer in its fist; the whole Foundry runs to its clock. Tick-Tock Press (round marks light on a chequer of floor tiles round you, then iron presses shoot down out of the dark and stamp them; the other tiles light next, then the first again, in time with its clock: step onto a tile that has just been stamped, or walk out of the chequer), Saw Blades (strips run from it past you to the wall and off it, then spinning cog saws roll along them and bounce off the wall; cover breaks them), Conveyor Belts (strips across the room turn into running belts that carry you along, red-hot ingots riding them; stand in the gaps or cross between ingots) and, below half health, Pendulum (strips across the room, then great crescent blades swing along them from wall to wall a few times; keep out of the marked strips until they stop) as its furnace burns white-hot, its clock races and two Clockwork Skeletons march out of the walls. The Pass screen pictures it beside the other four bosses only the Pass opens (art/ui/pass_boss_forgemaster.png).
- **The Star Eater** (version 268, [scripts/enemies/star_eater.gd](scripts/enemies/star_eater.gd)): the Starfall Abyss's own last boss (it was the Sporemother before). A giant skeleton with bones dark as the night sky, a fallen star burning in its ribs, a collar of night sky behind its skull, a crown of meteor shards and three little moons circling its head. Constellation (stars fall round you, then the lines between them strike), Gravity Well (it drags you in while a circle fills, then bursts; a dash is never pulled), Moon Sweep (moons spiral out of it across the room) and, below half health, Starfall (stars rain on the whole room). Met again it opens a Black Hole that pulls you while a constellation falls.
- **Elite skeletons:** golden, bigger and tougher versions that drop extra coins, more common on deeper floors.
- **Endless descent:** beat the king and carry your blessings down to the next floor, where skeletons get about 55% more health and 35% more damage per floor, and bosses even more. Descending heals 30%.
- **Endless loops (Crypt Pass):** below floor 5 the crypt comes round again in loops of five floors, each one darker and redder. After each loop's last boss you choose: BANK AND LEAVE (the run ends and pays its coins again, plus 25% more for each Omen) or DESCEND DEEPER, which first has you pick one of three **Omens** (three change the rules: the Omen of Graves makes fallen skeletons burst into bone spikes, the Omen of the Stalker sends a shade you can't hurt that glides in while you stand still, and the Omen of Night darkens the crypt so only your lantern lights it; the others bring tougher or harder-hitting skeletons, bigger waves or more golden elites). Omens stack and each adds +25% score. Between loops you get full health and 2 more rerolls, and the run saves on the end-of-loop screen so CONTINUE comes back to that choice. See [scripts/loops.gd](scripts/loops.gd).
- **Coins and the Armory:** skeletons drop gold you keep between runs. Spend it on permanent upgrades (health, damage, attack speed, move speed, luckier hearts, a free blessing each run, and a once-per-run Second Wind; 2,425 coins in all, later levels cost more). The Armory's **VAULT** tab sells ten new blessings (5,300 coins in all): Soul Siphon, Frozen Ground, Executioner, Flashfire, Bone Splinters and Overcharge, and since version 219 Opening Volley, Last Stand, Blood Rush and Chain Echo. Once bought, they can turn up in every run's blessing picks.
- **Music:** seven orchestral CC0 tracks from OpenGameArt, two for each floor theme (played one after the other) and a boss theme, credited in [audio/music/CREDITS.md](audio/music/CREDITS.md) and fetched by the Fetch CC0 music workflow. Music, Sounds, Graphics (High/Low), Screen shake (Full/Reduced/Off) and Vibration switches in the pause menu, also reachable from SETTINGS on the title screen. Screen shake also softens the close-up zoom, the flashes and the opening animation's shake. Low graphics turns off shadows and glow, and keeps only 2 of the 6 torch lights for smoother play on older phones. Until a player picks a setting, the game switches itself to Low if most of 8 seconds run under 45 frames a second, not counting each room's first 3 seconds (robot check: `slowphone`). The pause menu also shows the build, so testers can say which one they played.
- **Run report:** for players who send reports, the end screen has a COPY REPORT button. It copies the hero, damage taken from each enemy, blessings, room times and frame rate, to paste to Claude for balancing.
- **Grave Dash:** dash through a red enemy shot just before it hits for a PERFECT dodge: time slows, nearby shots shatter, the dash recharges and your hero fires a ring of 8 bolts back. Until you've landed 3 perfect dodges, the game slows down right before a shot hits and shows "DASH THROUGH IT!" (twice per run at most). Dash across a spike trap while it's armed and it fires on the skeletons around it instead.
- **Bone Chain:** kills close together build a multiplier (+0.1 per kill, +0.3 for elites, up to x3). It boosts coins and damage by +25% per full x1 (Marrow lets it climb to x4), and drains away 2.5 s after your last kill.
  The spine across the top lights one vertebra per +0.5 (bone-white, then gold, then red-hot), and shatters when the chain breaks.
- **Score:** every kill is worth points (10, elites 30, the boss 500) times the full chain. Specials also hit with the full chain, and a perfect dodge adds +0.5 and resets the timer. The best score is saved.
- **The Bone Road** ([scripts/bone_road.gd](scripts/bone_road.gd)): every room cleared adds a bone and every boss 4 more, in any mode, and bones are never lost. Steps along the road pay coins, Bone Drops and Lucky Starts, and the last one, at 1,000 bones, pays the **Bone Chain aura**, found nowhere else: a ring of vertebrae round the hero whose bones burn gold, then red-hot, as your Bone Chain grows in a run (its road screen shows it on your hero before you get there).
- **First run (the tutorial):** two easy rooms and the boss (version 169): room 1 teaches move and shoot, room 2 the dash (with one archer), and the boss room the special (which starts full) and the Bone Chain. Two waves a room, no bone spiders or bombers, and a King with half health; a killing hit on the tutorial floor leaves the hero on 1 health, so it can't be lost. Beating its boss ends it (and, since version 306, always lifts the hero to Mastery level 2), and Heroes and the Armory open. Floor 2 starts with three "Gifts for the road": since version 306, three different blessings that change how the hero fights (one of the hero's own, then shot, fire, frost or ward cards), not random number cards. The first run is saved like any other once its boss falls. "Tutorial run" in MODES replays it.
- **Title screen:** PLAY, then HEROES and ARMORY, then MODES (the Daily Crypt, Curses and the tutorial run, with a DAILY READY tag when today's Daily Crypt is waiting) and the CRYPT PASS.
- **Special moves:** kills fill a ring on the star button (bottom right). When it's full, tap it: the Rogue fires three rings of bolts (Shadow Volley), the Knight stuns everything nearby (Shield Slam), the Barbarian spins and heals (Whirlwind), and the Mage drops five meteors (Meteor). Perfect dodges and elites charge it faster. Press E on a keyboard.
- **Floor themes:** each floor has its own look and its own props: the Bone Crypt (floor 1, skull piles and candles; since version 277 an old crypt floor of dark slate flagstones with two rows of carved grave slabs (a skull and crossbones over lines of worn writing) and, in the middle, the Bone Chain: a ring of chain links made of bone round the Skeleton King's crowned skull, gold in its crown and candlelight in its eyes, under a cooler candlelit light, with candles burnt down on stone steps and heaps of bones along the walls; since version 265 its own cover too: blocks of ossuary wall, thigh bones laid ends out between rows of skulls with candles burning on top, instead of crates, and bone totems, thigh bones bound in iron with a red rag and a candle on a big skull, instead of barrels), the Frozen Ossuary (floor 2, blue torches, falling frost, icicles and ice crystals; since version 258 its own cover too: fallen hollow trunks sprouting glowcaps, with a skull looking out of one end and a bony hand reaching out of the other, instead of crates, and hollow tree stumps with glowing shelf fungus instead of barrels) and the **Sandfall Tomb** (floor 5, version 247: sandstone blocks carved with signs, a winged sun inlaid in the middle, painted frieze walls, obelisks, jars, painted coffins guarding the door, lotus columns and sand pouring in through cracks in the walls, with its own music and room sound; since version 260 its own cover too: rowboats lying keel up with a drowned sailor's legs sticking out from under them instead of crates, and old brass diving helmets with a skull looking out of the porthole instead of barrels), and the **Amethyst Mine** (floor 7, the second Crypt Pass floor, version 248: grey rock with amethyst veins, mine rails with a side track ending at a buffer, timber props along the walls, glowing crystal clusters, ore carts, boarded tunnels with lanterns and lumpy rock columns, with its own music and the sound of picks echoing; Drowned Skeletons climb out of its flooded shafts, and since version 272 its own Geode Guards stand watch there (it borrowed the Grotto's Rootbinders too before); since version 261 its own cover too: great geodes split open with amethyst glowing inside instead of crates, and heaps of ore with a pickaxe, a shovel and a lit lantern instead of barrels) and the **Whispering Library** (floor 8, the Lich's floor, version 249: herringbone parquet with worn rugs and a brass circle of glowing letters in the middle, bookcase walls and bookcase pillars, books that glow as they whisper, lecterns whose pages fly round them, library ladders, a tall clock and a great globe, with its own music and the sound of whispering and a ticking clock; Frost Wraiths, the ghosts of its readers, haunt it, and since version 271 its own Hush Keepers write there (it borrowed Cinder Skeletons too before); since version 262 its own cover too: the desks of dead scribes, heaped with books, an open book and a skull on the pages and candles still burning, instead of crates, and great hourglasses of glowing green sand instead of barrels) and the **Clockwork Foundry** (floor 9, version 250, the Forgemaster's floor since version 281 (the Cinder Colossus's before): riveted iron plates with rust and oil, long grates with the furnaces glowing under them, a great brass gear and two small ones turning in the floor, iron walls with copper pipes and trembling gauges, boilers with fire doors, gears turning on the walls, pumping pistons, iron pillars with turning brass collars and heaps of spare cogs, with its own music and the hiss of steam; pale moonstone plates drifted apart with stars and a nebula glowing far below in every gap, the floor crumbling away by the walls, a star gate in the middle (a window onto a turning spiral of stars under a silver seal), walls split by starlit cracks under a frieze of silver constellations, broken pillars whose pieces float apart, braziers of cold star fire with rocks circling over them, fallen stars, great chains hung from the dark and standing stones whose tops float, with its own music and the hum of the void; since version 269 its own Void Walkers stalk there (it borrowed the Grotto's Sporelings and Rootbinders before); since version 264 its own cover too: open moonstone coffins, broad at the shoulders, with their lids floating over them and a star-gazer lying inside in the night sky, holding a fallen star, instead of crates, and great cracked bells fallen out of the dark with starlight in the crack instead of barrels). See [scripts/floors.gd](scripts/floors.gd) and [scripts/props.gd](scripts/props.gd).
- **Blessings:** 27 shared blessings in four families (Shot, Flame, Frost, Bone) plus 3 for each hero that change how that hero fights (like Shadow Step for the Rogue or Buzzsaw for the Barbarian), each with its own icon and a rarity (common, rare, epic). Some only appear once you own the blessing they build on (Kindling and Pyre need Fire Shots, Frostbite and Deep Freeze need Frost Shots). Since version 307 most of them change what happens rather than a number: Bouncing Shots (Rogue and Mage: shots bounce off a wall or pillar once), Static Shots (every hit throws a spark of lightning on to the next skeleton, two jumps with a second pick), Spinning Bones (two bone blades circle the hero in every fight, three with a second pick), Blazing Dash (a trail of fire along the dash), Phantom Dash (the dash cuts every skeleton it passes through), Cold Snap (the special freezes every skeleton near the hero solid for 2 seconds), Kindling (a burning skeleton leaves a patch of fire where it falls), Frostbite (every third hit on a chilled skeleton freezes it solid) and Deep Freeze (a chilled skeleton shatters at 20% health; a boss takes +30%). Frozen solid, a skeleton stands in a block of ice and can't move or attack; bosses are only chilled. Eight shared cards are still plain numbers (damage, attack speed, critical chance, health, move speed, hearts, coins, chain time). Owning both halves of a synergy brings its gold card: Shatter (Fire + Frost), Bone Storm (Fan + Pierce), Ossuary (Ward + Vitality), Wildfire (Fire + Twin Shots: burning spreads), Glacier (Frost + Ward: a blocked hit freezes skeletons nearby) and Deadeye (Keen Eye + Pierce: critical hits fly through everything). One free reroll per run, plus one more on each new floor.
- **Place dangers** (hard review fix 5, one place per version): each new place gets a danger of its own on the floor. **Quicksand** in the Sandfall Tomb (version 284, [scripts/quicksand.gd](scripts/quicksand.gd)): two or three pits of dark sand in each room but the boss's, turning slowly round a dark throat behind a broken sandstone kerb, with bones half sunk in them circling inwards. The sand drags everything standing in it towards the middle (harder near the middle); walk out, or dash across, since a dash is never caught. Whoever reaches the middle sinks, is hurt and is thrown back out, and that goes for skeletons too: lead one across a pit and the sand takes a third of its health. **Mine gas** in the Amethyst Mine (version 285, [scripts/mine_gas.gd](scripts/mine_gas.gd)): two cracks at the foot of the walls of each room but the boss's glow green under a yellow warning sign and breathe out clouds of pale green firedamp every few seconds, which drift slowly into the room. The gas does nothing on its own, but any shot that flies through it, yours or a skeleton's, lights it: the cloud flares orange for half a second, then blows up, hurting everyone inside it (a skeleton loses nearly half its health) and lighting the clouds next to it. Shoot it when skeletons crowd into it; step out the moment it flares. **Rolling shelves** in the Whispering Library (version 286, [scripts/rolling_stacks.gd](scripts/rolling_stacks.gd)): in most rooms but the boss's, one of the library's free-standing bookcases (two in some rooms) stands on iron wheels on brass rails that cross the room from wall to wall. It waits at one wall; then its books rattle, arrows light along the rails the way it will go, and it rolls across to the other wall. It is moving cover, stopping shots both ways, and it pushes whatever is in its way ahead of it; whoever it pins on the far wall is crushed (hurt and thrown off the rails), skeletons too (they lose two fifths of their health). **Magnets** in the Clockwork Foundry (version 287, [scripts/electro_magnet.gd](scripts/electro_magnet.gd)): in most rooms but the boss's, two tall copper coils on iron plinths stand on the floor, one on each side, and take turns switching on. A coil hums and flickers first; once on, it glows blue and crackles for four seconds, and every shot in the room, yours and the skeletons', curves towards it in flight (so a shot can bend round cover, or miss), while everyone is dragged slowly towards it. Whoever is dragged right up to it is shocked: hurt and thrown back, skeletons too. (Version 287 also made the mine gas and the rolling shelves hit the hero less hard: on the deep floors a hit was a third of a hero's health.) **Loose plates** in the Starfall Abyss (version 288, [scripts/falling_plates.gd](scripts/falling_plates.gd)): in most rooms but the boss's, six to nine of the moonstone plates are cracked through: a shatter of jagged, branching cracks spreading from the point where each was struck, dark-edged with starlight glowing down them (version 289, drawn by a shader; they widen and burn white as the plate gives). Step on one (not in a dash) and it gives: it shakes and its cracks blaze white, and three quarters of a second later it falls into the void, leaving a hole of drifting stars for about five seconds before it floats back up. Whoever is on it as it falls, or walks into the hole, falls in: hurt, and climbing out at the nearest edge. Skeletons that step on a plate make it give too, and the void takes a third of their health. A dash carries the hero over a hole. From version 290 the free floors get dangers of their own too, one per version. **Black ice** in the Frozen Ossuary (floor 2, version 290, [scripts/ice_patch.gd](scripts/ice_patch.gd)): two or three sheets of dark, glossy ice in each room but the boss's (not in the first run), cracked and full of trapped bubbles, with a frosted rim and lumps of snow pushed up round the edge. On ice the hero slides: the stick only slowly turns and speeds them, and let go they keep gliding (still shooting), so it is easy to slide into a skeleton, a spike trap or a Frost Wraith's beam; a dash that ends on ice carries on as a long slide. Skeletons slip on it and walk at 60% speed, so leading them across it buys time. **Fire seams** in the Ember Catacomb (floor 3, version 291, [scripts/fire_seam.gd](scripts/fire_seam.gd)): two long charred cracks run across each room but the boss's (not in the first run), a jagged molten line down a black scorched band with broken rock along its lips. They take turns erupting: a seam glows brighter and spits embers for a second, then a wall of fire roars up along it for two seconds. The wall burns whoever stands in it, skeletons too (they lose almost a third of their health), and it burns up every shot that tries to cross it, the hero's and the skeletons', so while it is up it splits the room and is cover from archers. A dash leaps through it unhurt. **Bounce caps** in the Glowcap Grotto (floor 4, version 292, [scripts/bounce_cap.gd](scripts/bounce_cap.gd)): three or four great springy glowcaps squat on the floor of each room but the boss's (not in the first run), pink domes with lime arrows glowing in front of each to show which way it throws. Step on one and it squashes and flings the hero five metres through the air that way: no steering or shooting in the air, skeleton shots pass underneath, and the landing stamps the floor, knocking back the skeletons right there. It may land the hero in the middle of them. A skeleton that blunders onto one is thrown too and lands hard (a fifth of its health). A dash skims over a cap without setting it off. **The tide** in the Drowned Vault (floor 6, version 293, [scripts/tide_surge.gd](scripts/tide_surge.gd)): every ten seconds in each room but the boss's the sea breaks in. ## Art

The **Bone Spider** is our own model: [tools/blender/bone_spider.py](tools/blender/bone_spider.py) builds and animates it in Blender from code (run it with the `bpy` Python module) and writes `art/original/Bone_Spider.glb`.


The characters, weapons and dungeon props are Kay Lousberg's free CC0 **KayKit** packs
(Adventurers, Skeletons, Dungeon Remastered), kept in [art/kaykit](art/kaykit/). The
[Fetch KayKit art](.github/workflows/fetch-art.yml) workflow downloads them from the official
repositories. If a model is missing, the game falls back to simple placeholder shapes.

The logo lettering is **Fruktur** by The Fruktur Project Authors (SIL Open Font License, [fonts/OFL.txt](fonts/OFL.txt)). [tools/store/make_logo.py](tools/store/make_logo.py) draws it with its gold shine, dark edge and glow into [art/ui/logo.png](art/ui/logo.png), which the opening, the title screen and the share picture use, and the store banner gets a two-line version from the same script.

The chunky menu lettering (titles, buttons and big numbers) is **Lilita One** by Juan Montoreano (SIL Open Font License, [fonts/OFL-LilitaOne.txt](fonts/OFL-LilitaOne.txt)); body text uses Godot's built-in font.

## How it's built

Every push runs [.github/workflows/android-build.yml](.github/workflows/android-build.yml), which:
1. unpacks any KayKit zips,
2. lets a robot play a full run (it must beat the boss with no script errors),
3. exports a test app (`crypt-crawl.apk`, a release export signed with the test key, so it isn't
   debuggable) and checks it targets Android API 36, is 16 KB page aligned and not debuggable,
4. builds the signed Google Play release (`crypt-crawl.aab`) with Gradle, using the upload key
   (locked in `signing/upload-keystore.p12.enc`, unlocked by the `ANDROID_UPLOAD_PASSWORD` secret;
   without that secret the Play build is only a test and isn't published),
5. posts both on the
   [latest-build release](https://github.com/incorruptibleyt/AAA-GAME-DESIGN/releases/tag/latest-build).

`godot --headless -- autoplay god` (add `room=6` to jump to the boss, `shots=<folder>` for screenshots, `shop` to buy Armory upgrades first, `quittest` to test quitting a run, `gallery elites` for a line-up screenshot, `pass` to play with the Crypt Pass, `daily` for the Daily Crypt, `curses=brittle,famine` to switch curses on, `passmenu`/`cursemenu` for screenshots of those screens, `freeend floor=5` to test the end of the free game, `runs=3` to keep playing after a win, `killat=3` to close the game as room 3 starts and `continue` to press CONTINUE next time, `trial=mage` for a free hero trial, `dailymenu` for a Daily Crypt screenshot, `share` to print the Daily Crypt share text, `icons` for a picture of every blessing icon, `cards=riposte,quiver,frost` to offer exactly those cards first, `omens=graves,stalker,night` to play with those Omens from the first room, `vault` to own every Vault blessing, `heromenu` to picture every hero on the Heroes screen, `modesmenu` for the Modes screen, `newfoes floor=2 god` for the Frost Wraith and Cinder Skeleton up close, `props floor=3 god` for a floor's props, and `daily share die floor=2 room=3 chains=1.2,2,3` to picture the share card and save the share picture).
