Version 248: floor 7 is a new place, the Amethyst Mine
- The first Crypt Pass floor is now a worked-out mine full of glowing amethyst: rails across the floor with a side track, timber props along the walls, crystal clusters, ore carts, boarded tunnels with lanterns and rough rock columns.
- Its own music (cowbell, claves and plucked strings) and the sound of picks echoing in the dark.
