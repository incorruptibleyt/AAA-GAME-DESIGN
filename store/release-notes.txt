Version 275: the Amethyst Mine has its own boss
- Meet the Crystal Tyrant, the new floor 7 boss: a giant skeleton with amethyst grown through its bones and a crown of crystals.
- Spires of crystal burst up round you (hide behind them!), mine carts come thundering across the room, and a ring of crystals runs out across the floor.
- At half health it builds a crystal prison round you: get out before it shuts!
