Version 274: the Sandfall Tomb has its own boss
- Meet the Sand Pharaoh, the new floor 5 boss: a giant mummy king with a striped headcloth, a crook and flail, and a sun over its head.
- Its bandages lash out to reel you in (dash to snap them), sand pours from the ceiling and piles up to slow you, and scarabs swarm out of it.
- At half health its sun burns a beam across the room!
