Version 128: the Glowcap Grotto
- A new crypt on floor 4: a cave of glowing mushrooms with its own music.
- New boss, the Sporemother: she lobs spore pods, sends roots crawling after you and grows rings of mushrooms. In phase 2 the whole floor blooms.
- Two new skeletons: the Sporeling bursts into a spore cloud when it dies, and the Rootbinder sends roots that hold you in place (dash to break free).
- The five free floors now have four different bosses.
- Bug fixes.
