Version 277: floor 1 has a new look
- The Bone Crypt, the first floor, is now an old crypt of dark flagstones and carved grave slabs, lit by candles.
- In the middle of every room lies the Bone Chain: a ring of bone links round the Skeleton King's crowned skull, with candlelight in its eyes.
- Candles and heaps of bones along the walls instead of barrels and boxes.
