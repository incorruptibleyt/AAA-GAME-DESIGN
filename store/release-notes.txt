Version 262: the Whispering Library has its own cover
- On floor 8 the crates are now the desks of dead scribes: books stacked high, an open book with a skull resting on the pages, candles still burning and loose pages on the floor.
- The barrels are now great hourglasses in oak and brass, running with glowing green sand.
- They block movement and shots just like before.
- Floors 2 to 7 got their own cover in versions 256 to 261. Floors 9, 10 and 1 follow.
