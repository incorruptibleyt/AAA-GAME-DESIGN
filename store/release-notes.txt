Version 254: the Bone Road ends in a prize of its own
- The last step of the Bone Road, at 1,000 bones, now gives the Bone Chain aura to every hero, found nowhere else.
- It is a ring of vertebrae round your hero. In a run its bones light up one by one, gold then red-hot, as your Bone Chain grows.
- The Bone Road screen shows the prize on your hero, with how many bones are left.
