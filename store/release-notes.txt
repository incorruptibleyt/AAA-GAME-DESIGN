Version 252: every hero's level-20 aura has its own colour and shape
- Rogue (Nightlord): three violet shadow daggers circle you, leaving ghosts behind.
- Knight (Lionheart): a lion's mane of gold flame burns round your head.
- Barbarian (Worldbreaker): cyan lightning leaps off you and strikes the floor.
- Mage (Starsage): gold stars run round two green rings turning round you.
Before, all four were the same orange-gold column of light.
