Version 247: floor 5 is a new place, the Sandfall Tomb
- A royal tomb under the desert where the free game ends with the King and the Lich: sandstone carved with signs, a winged sun in the middle of the floor, painted walls, obelisks, jars, painted coffins by the door, lotus columns and sand pouring in through the walls.
