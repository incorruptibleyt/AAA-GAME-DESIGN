Version 276: the Whispering Library has its own boss
- Meet the Archivist, the new floor 8 boss: a skeleton scholar with page wings, glowing spectacles and a tome on a chain.
- It writes burning words across the floor, makes the footprints you left behind burst, and throws books off the shelves.
- At half health your own ink double climbs out of a page and copies your every step!
