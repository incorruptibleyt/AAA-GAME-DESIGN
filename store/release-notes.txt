Version 250: floor 9 is a new place, the Clockwork Foundry
- The Cinder Colossus's own floor is now an iron foundry: riveted plates, glowing furnace grates, a great brass gear turning in the floor, copper pipes and gauges on the walls, boilers, turning wall gears, pumping pistons and iron pillars.
- Its own music (clavinet, muted trumpet and a hammering beat) and the hiss of steam.
- Floors 7, 8 and 9 are no longer the first three floors relit: each is a place of its own.
