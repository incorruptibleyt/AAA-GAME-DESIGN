Version 241: Omens that change the rules
- Omen of Graves: fallen skeletons burst into bone spikes a moment later. Step away, or lead skeletons over the graves.
- Omen of the Stalker: a shade you can't hurt glides in while you stand still. Attack, step away, attack.
- Omen of Night: the crypt goes dark and only your lantern lights the way.
- Cursed Doors can bring these Omens too.
- Your first blessing pick changes how you fight (v240).
- The Blood Moon is up until Dec 3.
