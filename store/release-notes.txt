Version 280: the Free Dive
- Not sure about the Crypt Pass? Dive into floor 6 once for free: the Drowned Vault, its own skeletons and its boss, the Tidecaller, with 5 blessings to start.
- Find FREE DIVE on the Crypt Pass screen (the title's Crypt Pass button says TRY FLOOR 6 FREE).
- The dive counts once you reach the Tidecaller, so a fall before that lets you try again.
- The dive never touches your saved run, records or score.
