Version 249: floor 8 is a new place, the Whispering Library
- The Lich's own floor is now a library of forbidden books: herringbone floors, old rugs, a brass circle of glowing letters, bookcases for walls and pillars, lecterns with their pages flying round them, ladders, a tall clock and a great globe.
- Its own music (harpsichord, harp and soft voices) and the sound of whispering and a ticking clock.
