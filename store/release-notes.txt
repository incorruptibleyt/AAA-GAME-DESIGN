Version 281: the Forgemaster
- Floor 9, the Clockwork Foundry, has its own boss: the Forgemaster, a giant smith with a furnace in its ribs and a great clock behind its head.
- Dodge its presses in time with its clock, its saw blades that bounce off the walls and its conveyor belts of red-hot ingots.
- Below half health it swings giant pendulums and calls its Clockwork Skeletons.
